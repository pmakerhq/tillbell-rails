
Tillbell is installed. What is left happens on Google's side.

1. Put the two values Google gives you in your credentials
   (bin/rails credentials:edit, or --environment production):

     tillbell:
       # The whole JSON key file of the service account that calls Google.
       # Keep the "|" and the two-space indentation.
       google_service_account: |
         { "type": "service_account", "client_email": "<tillbell-api>@<project>.iam.gserviceaccount.com", ... }
       # The service account Pub/Sub signs its notifications with.
       pubsub_service_account_email: <tillbell-push>@<project>.iam.gserviceaccount.com

2. Set config.google.package_name in config/initializers/tillbell.rb.

3. Follow "Google Play setup" for every click (Play Console, service accounts, Pub/Sub). Allow
   up to a day for Google to apply the permissions you grant:
   https://github.com/pmakerhq/tillbell-rails#google-play-setup

4. Check it:

     bin/rails tillbell:check
     ONLINE=1 PLANS=premium:monthly bin/rails tillbell:check    # also asks Google
